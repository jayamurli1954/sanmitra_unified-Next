Privacy Policy

Version: 2.0   Last updated: 26 November 2026   Effective: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data LegalMitra collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

1. Who is responsible for your data

LegalMitra is operated by Muralidhar, trading as SanMitra Technologies, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("we", "us"). For your own account data, we are the Data Fiduciary.

Advocates' and professionals' client data: when you upload personal data of your clients or others for your professional work, you are the Data Fiduciary for that data and we act as your Data Processor, processing it only to provide LegalMitra to you. See section 7 of the Terms of Service.

Contact for privacy matters: Muralidhar — legalmitra@sanmitratech.in.

2. Our main promises

  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not use your queries, documents or matter data to train or fine-tune AI models, and our AI providers' terms do not allow them to train on it.
  • Each customer's data is kept separate; one firm's documents are never shown to another.
  • Data is kept only for your plan's retention period (section 6), then deleted.

4. Consent and legal basis

  • We process your account data and content on the basis of the consent you give when you sign up and accept these terms, for the purposes listed in section 3.
  • Some processing is allowed by law without consent — for example, to comply with a legal obligation or a court order (DPDP Act, section 7).
  • You can withdraw consent at any time (section 8). Withdrawing does not affect processing already done, but we may then be unable to provide the service.
  • We will ask for separate consent before using your data for any new purpose not listed here.

7. Where data is processed

Our servers are in Singapore, and some providers above are in the United States or other countries. The DPDP Act permits transfers outside India except to countries the Government restricts; we will follow any such restriction. We choose providers with strong security and contractual protections.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Access — ask for a summary of your personal data and how we process it, and who we have shared it with;
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erase — ask us to delete your data, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • Withdraw consent at any time;
  • Nominate a person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to;
  • Complain to us, and if not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use these rights, email legalmitra@sanmitratech.in. We may ask you to confirm your identity. If your request concerns data that an advocate or firm uploaded about you, we will pass it to them, as they decide how that data is used.

9. Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for all connections.
  • Access controls, so each user and organisation sees only its own data.
  • Only the minimum relevant extracts are sent to AI providers; for matter papers you can keep original documents with you and share only approved extracts.
  • Restricted administrative access and security logging.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and review our safeguards periodically.

10. Data breaches

If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India without delay, as the DPDP Act and Rules require, with what happened, what data was involved, and what you can do. For client data we process for professionals, we will inform the professional so they can meet their obligations.

11. Children

LegalMitra is for adults. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create an account. If we learn that a child has an account, we will delete it.

12. Cookies and browser storage

We use only what the service needs: browser storage to keep you signed in and remember your settings. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not currently use third-party analytics.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices or the law change. For material changes we will notify you by email or in-app at least 30 days before they take effect, and ask for fresh consent where required.
